# Ideas (not scheduled, just parked)

- **Shuffle mode** — pick a category or a set of sounds (e.g. all nature) and each wake plays a different one. Probably the best effort-to-delight ratio of anything here.
- **Fresh sounds on schedule** — connect an Eversince account; once a day or week it generates new sounds in the categories you like, so the library quietly grows and there's something new waiting. Needs audio support in the Eversince prompt-mode API first.
- **Interactive wake** — a voice in the app that greets you on wake, or asks two or three quick sharp questions to switch your brain on before work. Wake-up ritual, not just a sound.
- **More wake patterns** — different sounds for different times of day, weekdays vs weekends, first wake of the morning vs every wake.
